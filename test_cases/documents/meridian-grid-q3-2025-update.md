### Page 2 — Financing and Outlook (extract)

**Bond issuance.** On 2 July 2025 Meridian Grid Energy SE placed a green bond with a nominal value of €2.75 billion and a term of ten years. The bond carries a fixed coupon of 3.875% per annum, payable semi-annually on 2 January and 2 July. Proceeds are allocated exclusively to grid expansion and renewable connections.

In March 2025 the company had already issued a subordinated hybrid bond of €1.25 billion with a fixed coupon of 4.125% per annum, payable annually.

**Grid investment programme.** The Supervisory Board has approved a total investment of €6.25 billion in the distribution grid for the period 2025 to 2029. Of the €1,384 million capital expenditure in the first nine months of 2025, €1,102 million related to this programme.

**Outlook 2025.** For the full year, Meridian expects adjusted EBITDA of between €3.0 billion and €3.2 billion and net financial debt of no more than €15.5 billion at 31 December 2025.
