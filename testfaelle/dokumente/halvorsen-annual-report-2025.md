**Note 4. Restatement of prior-year revenue**

Following a review of contract terms, fuel surcharges collected on behalf of third-party hauliers are now presented net of the related cost. Revenue for 2024, as originally reported at £1,211.8 million, has been restated to £1,197.3 million, a reduction of £14.5 million. The restatement has no effect on operating profit, profit before tax or earnings per share.

**Note 7. Dividends**

The interim dividend was paid on 3 October 2025. The proposed final dividend is subject to shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting on 14 May 2026 and will be paid on 5 June 2026 to shareholders on the register on 24 April 2026.
